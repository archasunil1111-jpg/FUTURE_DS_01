# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Future Interns - Data Science & Analytics Task 1

This project analyzes business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, category performance, regional performance, and overall profitability.

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand business sales performance and identify areas where the business can focus to support growth.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Superstore Sales Dataset
- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard contains:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Sales Trend Over Time
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Region filter

## Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately **2.30M**.
- Total profit is approximately **286.40K**.
- Total quantity sold is approximately **38K units**.
- The **West** region recorded the highest sales at **725.46K** and the highest profit at **108.42K**.
- The **East** region recorded **678.78K** in sales and **91.52K** in profit.
- The **Central** region generated **510.24K** in sales and **39.71K** in profit.
- The **South** region generated **391.72K** in sales and **46.75K** in profit.

## Recommendations

- Continue monitoring the West and East regions because they currently generate the highest sales and profit.
- Investigate the lower profit relative to sales in the Central region by reviewing discounts and product-level margins.
- Monitor top-selling products to support inventory availability and continued revenue generation.
- Review lower-performing regions and categories to identify opportunities for targeted sales strategies.
